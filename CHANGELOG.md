## [0.1.0]

### Added

- FreeCAD workbench registration.
- Assembly scanning and part registry.
- Editable part metadata.
- Spreadsheet-backed metadata persistence with in-memory fallback.
- BOM generation.
- CSV and optional XLSX export.
- BOM persistence into FreeCAD spreadsheets.
- TechDraw auto-balloon planning and best-effort creation.
- Unit and integration-like tests using FreeCAD test doubles.
